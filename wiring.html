Exact wiring

PCM1808 stereo ADC input, two PCM5102 DACs, SSD1306 OLED and EC11 encoder.

Power and ground

RailConnect to
5 V, 2 A recommendedESP32 5V/VIN, PCM1808 VCC, both PCM5102 VIN
GNDESP32, PCM1808, both PCM5102, OLED, encoder common
3.3 VOLED VCC, PCM5102 XSMT (unmute)

Use one common ground. Keep I2S clocks short, add 100 nF + 10 uF at each codec, and keep DAC outputs away from the Wi-Fi antenna.

GPIO0 is a boot strapping pin. PCM1808 SCKI is high impedance, so boot is normally fine. If your board loads the BOOT pin, remove that capacitor or use a PCM1808 module with its own oscillator and set PIN_I2S0_MCLK to -1.

On N16R8, GPIO19/20 are native USB, GPIO26-32 are flash and GPIO33-37 are octal PSRAM. They are not used.

Final notes

Both PCM5102 outputs are line level (~2 Vrms). Never connect them directly to a speaker. Use a power amplifier or active speakers.

If the I2S clocks ring or a DAC locks unreliably, add a 33-100 ohm series resistor at the ESP32 end of MCLK/BCK/LRCK.
